Vision Mission Program Specific Outcomes Courses Offered Top Recruiters
VISION :

To be a center of excellence in computer engineering education and research, empowering students to innovate, lead, and solve real-world problems through advanced technology and ethical practices.
MISSION :
  • To provide a strong foundation in computer engineering principles with hands-on, project-based learning.
  • To cultivate innovation and problem-solving skills for designing efficient computing solutions.
  • To promote research, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning in emerging technologies.
  • To develop ethical and socially responsible engineers ready for global challenges.
  • To strengthen industry-academia partnerships for enhanced employability and real-world exposure.
PROGRAM SPECIFIC OUTCOMES (PSOs) :
  1. Apply computer engineering principles to design and develop efficient software and hardware solutions.
  2. Analyze complex computing problems and innovate effective algorithmic and system-levelsolutions.
  3. Demonstrate proficiency in emerging technologies like AI, cybersecurity, and cloud computing.
  4. Uphold professional ethics and contribute responsibly to society through engineering practices.
